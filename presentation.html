The Healthcare Challenge

Fragmented Data & Congested OPDs

Traditional healthcare delivery in India suffers from three systemic bottlenecks.

Siloed & Lost Records

Health records are trapped in disparate hospitals, paper folders, and diagnostic clinics.

  • Redundant & expensive re-testing
  • Missed past surgeries and allergies
  • Blind spots in emergency care

OPD Queue Congestion

Consultation time is consumed by routine administrative data entry rather than care.

  • 40% time spent transcribing history
  • Long waiting lines for patients
  • No pre-triaging of high-risk red flags

Zero Patient Consent

Legacy electronic health systems lack patient privacy controls and audit accountability.

  • Data shared without patient approval
  • No time-bounded permissions
  • Absence of tamper-proof audit trails

Life-Saving Protocol

Emergency "Break-Glass" Access

Emergency override protocol balancing immediate clinical care with complete auditability.

The ER Trauma Dilemma

When an unconscious patient arrives with acute trauma, they cannot provide digital consent on a phone. Denying access could be fatal.

  • Needs immediate blood group & drug allergy check
  • Needs prior surgical and cardiac history

The HealthBridge Resolution

Doctor can bypass standard consent by providing mandatory clinical justification (e.g. *"Acute Trauma ER — Patient unconscious"*).

  • Instant unlocking of life-saving medical chart
  • Immutable high-priority audit event generated immediately
  • Push notification & permanent record in patient's portal
